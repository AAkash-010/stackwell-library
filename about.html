Our story

A reading room for the whole town

Stackwell Library opened as a single borrowed room above the old post office. Seventy years on, it's grown into a full community library system — but the goal hasn't changed: a free, quiet place for anyone to read, research, and belong.

Today Stackwell holds a growing print collection, a local history archive, four bookable study rooms, and a members' portal for holds and renewals. We're staffed by librarians, not algorithms — every recommendation on our shelves was chosen by someone who reads the book first.

We believe access to information shouldn't depend on income. Membership is free for residents, our reference desk is unrestricted, and our study spaces are open to students, job-seekers, and anyone else who needs a table and some quiet.
